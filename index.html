Idea =equals experiment.

Equals Imaginarium is where curiosity gets a workbench. We take “what if?” and turn it into something you can measure, build, break and build again.

The concept

Imagination, held to account.

The “equals” is the promise: every idea has to balance against reality. We sketch boldly, then test honestly — with prototypes, measurements and notes anyone can follow.

  1. 01

    Imagine

    Start with a question worth chasing, however odd it sounds.

  2. 02

    Build

    Make the simplest thing that could possibly answer it.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Let the data argue back. Record what actually happened.

  4. 04

    Share

    Publish the build notes, the wins and the glorious failures.
