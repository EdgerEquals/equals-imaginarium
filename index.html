An interactive 3D space with no words. You float inside it. Drag to look around. On a touch screen, drag on the left half to drift and on the right half to look, and pinch to float forward or back. With a mouse, scroll to float forward or back and right-drag to drift. On a keyboard, W A S D moves, arrow keys look and move, Q and E sink and rise. Thousands of small lights sweep past on golden-ratio orbits around a nebula, while a steel sphere, two rings, an equals sign and a coral bead drift and bounce inside an invisible bounded space ahead of you.
